Recall Privacy Policy

Your cards stay on your phone.

Who we are

Recall is a flashcard app made by Pranjal Singh, who publishes apps on Google Play as Jugaadify. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Recall on Android.

The short version

Recall does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

What stays on your phone

Your decks, cards, study progress and settings are saved in Recall's private storage on your phone, where other apps can't read them. Android cloud backup is turned off for Recall, so this data is not copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app, or Settings > Erase all data, deletes it.

Files you import or export

When you import a CSV or backup file, Android's file picker gives Recall only the file you choose, and Recall reads it on your phone. When you export a backup or CSV, Android's share sheet sends it where you choose, such as a messaging app or a cloud drive. From then on, the app or service you picked handles that file under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

Recall does not ask for internet, camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage access.

Third parties

Recall contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. Its fonts are bundled inside the app, so nothing is downloaded while you use it. Google Play may collect information when you download or update Recall; that is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Children

Recall does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and at this web address, with a new effective date.
